# Nature-Complementary Product Series

The Nature-Complementary Product Series defines concrete implementation and product concepts derived from Natural Complementary Science.

These concepts translate the framework into cautious, testable directions. They are not finished products and should not be described as scientifically proven, deployment-ready, or universally effective.

## 1. Mobility and Urban Heat: UHV

Repository:

- [Ultimate Hybrid Vehicle UHV](https://github.com/InchaComisho/Ultimate-Hybrid-Vehicle-UHV)

Role:

UHV is the first representative mobility product concept. It treats vehicles as mobile environmental support nodes that may assist local heat mitigation, auxiliary energy recovery, rainwater use, and parking heat-soak reduction under validated conditions.

Classification:

- Natural Complementary Science
- Mobility
- Urban Heat Mitigation
- Climate-Adaptive Infrastructure
- Open Invention
- Prototype Candidate

## 2. Soil and Microorganisms: Soil Repair Autonomous Unit

A future candidate product concept for supporting living soil recovery.

Possible functions:

- organic matter collection
- compost or humus input support
- soil moisture sensing
- microbial condition monitoring
- seedling protection
- small-scale regenerative land maintenance

This should be presented as a future concept requiring ecological and safety validation.

## 3. Ocean and Thermal Circulation: Ocean Breathing Autonomous Buoy

A future candidate product concept for ocean circulation support and marine monitoring.

Possible functions:

- vertical water-mixing support
- dissolved oxygen monitoring
- nutrient and plankton observation
- localized cooling and circulation experiments
- marine ecological safety monitoring

This should be presented as a cautious ocean research and monitoring concept, not a deployment-ready geoengineering device.

## 4. Urban Heat and Water: Rainwater-Mist Cooling Node

A future candidate product concept for buildings, parking lots, bus stops, and transit corridors.

Possible functions:

- rainwater collection
- filtration and UV treatment
- fine mist cooling
- humidity-aware control
- pedestrian safety monitoring
- heat-stress reduction experiments

This should be evaluated as a local climate-adaptation candidate that requires water-quality, humidity, visibility, and public-safety validation.

## 5. Forest and Organic Circulation: Leaf-to-Humus Support Unit

A future candidate product concept for converting fallen leaves and safe organic matter into soil-recovery inputs.

Possible functions:

- leaf collection
- contamination screening
- composting support
- moisture control
- humus distribution
- forest-edge restoration support

This should be validated for ecological safety, contamination risk, local regulation, and long-term soil effects.

## Summary

The Nature-Complementary Product Series translates the principles of Natural Complementary Science into cautious, testable implementation candidates.

These concepts are not finished products. They are proposed as open-invention directions that require engineering, ecological, legal, safety, and regional validation.
