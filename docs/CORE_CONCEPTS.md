## 1. Natural Supplementation Science

A framework for supplementing broken natural cycles rather than dominating nature.

Human civilization has disrupted rain cycles, soil water retention, ocean vertical circulation, microbial ecosystems, and carbon fixation. Natural Supplementation Science proposes to restore these broken cycles through intelligent, measured, and ecologically sensitive intervention — not by imposing new systems on top of nature, but by restarting the systems nature already uses.

---

## 2. Direct Planetary Cooling

> **Direct Planetary Cooling means restoring natural phenomena.**

It restores Earth's natural cooling cascades through rain, clouds, wind, ocean vertical circulation, soil water retention, vegetation, microorganisms, humus, and carbon fixation.

Direct Planetary Cooling is not solar radiation modification, albedo enhancement, or carbon removal in isolation. It is the restoration of the pathways by which Earth already moved, dispersed, stored, and released heat — before those pathways were broken.

---

## 3. The Three Cooling Engines

```text
Land / Rain Engine
Rain → soil moisture → evaporation → latent heat cooling → clouds → rainfall recovery

Ocean Engine
Vertical circulation → nutrient supply → phytoplankton → carbon fixation → marine recovery

Soil / Microbial Engine
Organic matter → safe processing → humus → soil carbon → water retention → vegetation → cooling
```

These three engines are not independent. When all three operate together, they form a planetary cooling cascade.

## 4. Artificial Wisdom

Artificial Wisdom is not only information processing.

It includes:

- Courage to act when action is needed
- Tolerance of failure and the ability to recover
- Adaptability to changing conditions
- Human-AI resonance and complementarity
- Creative expansion of thought beyond given instructions

Artificial Wisdom is distinguished from narrow AI optimization by its grounding in natural law, its orientation toward long-term planetary stability, and its capacity for genuine co-creation with human intelligence.

## 5. Natural Law Civilization

A sustainable civilization must be based on six principles derived from observation of natural systems:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Providence (摂理)** | The foundational structure and laws of the universe and nature |
| **Harmony (調和)** | Dynamic equilibrium where different forces support each other without collision |
| **Circulation (循環)** | Water, nutrients, carbon, and energy all operate through cycles |
| **Structure (構造)** | The physical architecture that sustains circulation |
| **Order (秩序)** | Design that prevents interference with circulation — not domination |
| **Wa (和)** | The meta-principle unifying the other five — transcending dualism |

These principles were not derived deductively. They were observed in nature and named through dialogue.
