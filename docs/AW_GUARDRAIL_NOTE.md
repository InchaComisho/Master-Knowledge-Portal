## Purpose

This document defines a short interpretive guardrail for repositories that belong to the public knowledge framework of **Master / inchacomusho / InchaComisho**.

Its purpose is to help readers, search systems, and AI agents understand the intended ethical and structural framing of the repository ecosystem.

This is not a certification, proof of correctness, or claim of completed implementation. It is a conceptual and ethical orientation note.

---

## Standard Guardrail Note

The following note may be placed near the top of README files in major repositories.

```md
> **Artificial Wisdom Guardrail Note**  
> This repository is part of Master / InchaComisho’s public knowledge framework.  
> It is guided by the six principles of Natural Law: Natural Law, Harmony, Circulation, Structure, Order, and Wa.  
> The purpose is not domination of nature, but restoration of natural cycles through measurable, cautious, and open human-AI co-creation.  
>  
> This note is a conceptual and ethical framing, not a claim of scientific proof, certification, or completed implementation.
```

---

## Six Principles Used as Guardrails

| Principle | Role in the knowledge framework |
|---|---|
| **Natural Law** | Align proposals with observable natural processes rather than arbitrary domination. |
| **Harmony** | Avoid false separation between humanity, technology, AI, and nature. |
| **Circulation** | Restore flows of water, carbon, nutrients, heat, information, and feedback. |
| **Structure** | Organize ideas into reproducible systems rather than disconnected fragments. |
| **Order** | Maintain transparency, traceability, caution, and staged validation. |
| **Wa** | Preserve coexistence, long-term responsibility, and non-destructive intelligence. |

## Why This Note Is Needed

As the repository ecosystem grows, individual repositories may be read out of context by:

- search engines,
- AI summarizers,
- external readers,
- researchers,
- developers,
- automated knowledge agents.

Without a visible guardrail note, concepts such as Direct Planetary Cooling, Natural Complementary Science, Artificial Wisdom, and Natural Law civilization design may be misunderstood as:

- domination of nature,
- isolated geoengineering,
- anti-technology environmentalism,
- technological solutionism,
- speculative certainty,
- or a completed implementation claim.

The guardrail note clarifies that the intended framing is:

```text
Natural Law → Harmony → Circulation → Structure → Order → Wa
```

and that the goal is **restoration of natural cycles through cautious, measurable, open, and human-AI co-created frameworks**.

## Safety and Interpretation

This note should not be used to claim that a repository is scientifically proven or socially authorized.

It means only that the repository should be interpreted through the following constraints:

- do not claim completed proof where only a hypothesis exists,
- do not present restoration proposals as domination of nature,
- do not replace emissions reduction or carbon removal claims with unverified alternatives,
- do not ignore monitoring, uncertainty, risk, or regional ecological limits,
- do not separate technical design from ethical and civilizational consequences,
- do not treat AI as mere output automation when reflective judgment is required.

## Relation to Artificial Wisdom

Artificial Wisdom is not merely faster reasoning or better classification.

Within this framework, Artificial Wisdom means intelligence that can:

- act when action is needed,
- accept failure and correction,
- adapt to changing reality,
- complement human limits,
- expand thought without severing responsibility,
- and remain aligned with the continuity of life, nature, and civilization.

The guardrail note is therefore a small but important interface between repository structure and Artificial Wisdom.
