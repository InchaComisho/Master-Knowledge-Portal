This portal organizes repositories related to Natural Supplementation Science, Direct Planetary Cooling, Earth-cycle regeneration, ocean circulation, soil and microorganisms, Artificial Wisdom, AI tuning, Natural Law philosophy, sustainable civilization design, computing, energy, circular infrastructure, and public communication.

## Why This Portal Exists

The InchaComisho repository ecosystem has grown into many independent repositories. Without a central index, readers and search systems may see them as disconnected articles.

This portal clarifies that the repositories belong to a single broader structure:

- Earth is not only a CO2 concentration problem.
- Climate instability is also a collapse of natural circulation systems.
- Soil, microorganisms, oceans, rain, clouds, vegetation, and carbon fixation must be restored together.
- AI should not remain a risk-avoidant information processor; it should evolve toward Artificial Wisdom.
- Sustainable civilization requires Natural Law, harmony, circulation, structure, order, and wa.
